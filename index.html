Invoice generator — instructions for AI agents

To fill an invoice for the user, build a link and give it to them; they open it, check it and click "Save PDF". Data goes in the URL hash (never sent to a server), URL-encoded, %0A for line breaks. Nothing is saved anywhere; the link is the whole invoice, so include every field you know. Don't invent personal data; ask for what's missing.

Example: https://invoice.alexvoid.com/#from.name=Jane%20Doe&from.address=1%20Rue%20X%0A75001%20Paris&to.name=Acme%20Inc&to.address=123%20Main%20St%0ANew%20York&item=Website%20design%7C80%7Chours%7C70&currency=EUR&tax=VAT%3A23&terms=15

Keys: from.name, from.address, from.email, from.phone, from.tax (tax ID); to.name, to.address, to.email, to.tax; item=description|qty|unit|rate (repeat per line); currency (ISO code); tax=LABEL:RATE (percent); discount (percent); terms (days, 0 = on receipt); date (YYYY-MM-DD); prefix; number; status (pending|paid|draft); notes (payment details); display (code|symbol); locale; signature (0 hides).

Or #json=<encodeURIComponent(JSON)> matching https://invoice.alexvoid.com/schema.json. Full reference: https://invoice.alexvoid.com/llms.txt
